Product Demonstrations & Complete Use Guides

Choose a product to see its purpose, recommended application sequence and important precautions. The matching demonstration poster is shown with each product guide; always follow the approved pack label.

BIKE & RIDER CARE · 250 ML

Helmet Cleaner & Odour Remover

Purpose: Foam cleaning and freshening of compatible inner helmet foam and fabric lining.

  1. Use only when the helmet will not be needed for at least 1 hour.
  2. Place the helmet in an open, well-ventilated area. Never spray while wearing it.
  3. Spray evenly over compatible inner foam and fabric lining; allow the rich foam to spread through the treated area.
  4. Allow about 1 minute for the cleaning action.
  5. Keep the helmet unused for 30–45 minutes so the wetness can dry completely. Wear only when the lining is fully dry.

Important: Avoid direct application to the visor, electronics, ventilation components and chin strap. Check compatibility on a small area first.

CABIN CARE · 200 ML

Odour Remover & Cabin Sanitizer

Purpose: Help reduce unpleasant cabin odours and leave a fresh fragrance.

  1. Use only in a parked vehicle in a safe, open and well-ventilated location.
  2. Start the vehicle and set the A/C to full cooling and full circulation only if this method is expressly permitted by the product label.
  3. Place the can upright on a stable floor area, preferably near the centre of the cabin.
  4. Activate the spray only as directed on the can. Close doors and windows only for the label-approved treatment period.
  5. The supplied demonstration describes an 8–10 minute circulation period. After the can is empty, wait about 5 minutes, then open the doors and ventilate thoroughly.

Critical safety: No driver, passengers, children or pets may remain inside. Do not leave the vehicle unattended. Do not use automatic-release fogging unless the final can label explicitly approves it. Follow all re-entry and ventilation instructions on the label.

GLASS & VISIBILITY · CURRENT CATALOGUE SIZE: 100 ML

Windscreen Additive

Purpose: Washer-fluid additive to help remove dirt, dust and road stains and support a clear windscreen.

  1. Measure 25 ml of BrightMoto Windscreen Additive.
  2. Mix with 2 litres of clean water.
  3. Pour the prepared mixture into a compatible windscreen washer reservoir. Do not overfill.
  4. Operate the washer and wipers to distribute the cleaning solution across the glass.

Check first: Confirm washer-system compatibility and the correct product concentration on the final label. The supplied poster artwork shows a 250 ml pack while the existing website catalogue lists 100 ml; confirm the actual pack label before changing the listed size.

INTERIOR & EXTERIOR CLEANING · CURRENT CATALOGUE SIZE: 250 ML

Multi Surface Cleaner

Purpose: Cleaning compatible plastic, acrylic and other washable automotive surfaces.

Suggested applications shown: Wheel arches, wheel trims/caps, underwheel surface areas, bumpers, skirtings and door claddings.

  1. Test on a small, inconspicuous area first.
  2. Spray onto a clean microfibre cloth, or directly onto a suitable surface if the product label permits.
  3. Gently wipe to loosen and remove dirt, grime and road film.
  4. Wipe away residue with a clean section of cloth and allow the surface to dry.

Check first: Do not use on screens, delicate finishes or surfaces not approved by the label. The supplied poster shows a 500 ml pack while the existing website catalogue lists 250 ml; confirm the actual pack label before changing the listed size.

PAINT FINISHING · 250 ML

Polish & Wax Nano Spray

Purpose: Quick detailing to enhance gloss and leave a smooth-looking finish on compatible painted surfaces.

  1. Prepare two clean microfibre cloths: Cloth 1 for application and Cloth 2 for buffing.
  2. Gently spray the product onto Cloth 1, not directly onto the vehicle.
  3. Apply evenly to the required surface area.
  4. Wait approximately 2 minutes.
  5. Use clean, dry Cloth 2 to buff the area to a shine.

Important: Test first on an inconspicuous area and avoid hot surfaces or direct sunlight. The product may improve the appearance of very light marks; it does not repair scratches or replace paint correction.

INTERIOR TRIM CARE · 250 ML

Dashboard Dresser

Purpose: Clean, dress and refresh the appearance of compatible dashboard and interior trim surfaces.

  1. Prepare two microfibre cloths: Cloth 1 for application and Cloth 2 for the final wipe.
  2. Spray a small amount onto Cloth 1; do not spray directly onto the dashboard.
  3. Apply evenly over the compatible dashboard surface.
  4. Wait approximately 30 seconds.
  5. Use clean, dry Cloth 2 to wipe and finish the surface.

Important: Keep product away from pedals, steering controls, instrument displays, touchscreens and glass. Avoid surfaces where a slippery finish could affect vehicle control.

CAR & BIKE WASH · 250 ML

Premium Luxe Snow Shampoo

Purpose: Concentrated, pH-balanced wash shampoo for routine exterior cleaning of cars and bikes.

Correct dilution ratio: 1:200.

  1. Measure 5 ml of shampoo for every 1 litre of water.
  2. For a 5-litre wash bucket, use 25 ml of shampoo and add 5 litres of water.
  3. Apply the diluted solution using suitable wash equipment or a clean wash mitt.
  4. Work over the exterior to loosen dirt and road grime without allowing the solution to dry on the surface.
  5. Rinse thoroughly with clean water and dry using a suitable microfibre towel.

Important: Use the 1:200 dilution; do not substitute a stronger concentration. Wash in a shaded area and follow the product label.

Pack-size note: the website catalogue retains its existing listed sizes where the supplied poster artwork differs. Confirm final printed labels and pack sizes before publishing any revised size claims. Product claims, material compatibility and aerosol instructions must match the approved formulation and label.
