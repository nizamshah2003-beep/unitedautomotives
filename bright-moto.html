BrightMoto application guides

Product Demonstrations & How to Use

Practical product-use information for selected BrightMoto products. Always read the pack label and check material compatibility before use.

CAR & BIKE WASH

Premium Luxe Shampoo

1:200 dilution — 5 ml shampoo per 1 litre of water; 25 ml per 5 litres. Apply using suitable wash equipment and rinse thoroughly.

PAINT FINISHING

Nano Spray Polish

Use two microfibre cloths. Spray onto Cloth 1 and apply to the required surface. Wait 2 minutes, then buff with a clean, dry Cloth 2. Minor marks may appear less noticeable; deeper scratches need professional correction.

INTERIOR TRIM

Dashboard Dresser

Spray onto Cloth 1, apply evenly over compatible dashboard surfaces and wait about 30 seconds. Finish with a second clean, dry microfibre cloth. Keep product off pedals, steering controls and screens.

GLASS & VISIBILITY

Windscreen Additive

Mix 25 ml additive with 2 litres of clean water and add to a compatible windscreen washer tank. Operate the washer to help clean dirt, dust and road stains from the glass.

EXTERIOR SURFACES

MultiSurface Cleaner

For compatible plastic, acrylic and washable automotive surfaces: wheel arches, wheel trims/caps, underwheel areas, bumpers, skirtings and door claddings. Apply to a cloth or suitable surface, wipe gently and finish clean.

CABIN CARE

Odour Remover & Cabin Sanitizer

Use the 8–10 minute A/C circulation method only if expressly approved by the final product label. Park in a safe, open, well-ventilated area, attend the vehicle throughout, and keep all occupants, children and pets outside. Follow label ventilation instructions before re-entry.

HELMET & RIDER CARE

Helmet Cleaner

Use only when the helmet will not be needed for at least 1 hour. Apply in an open, ventilated area to compatible inner foam/fabric lining. Keep unused for 30–45 minutes and ensure it is completely dry before wearing. Never spray while wearing the helmet.

Product performance, sanitizing claims, material compatibility and aerosol operating instructions must match the final approved formulation and label. The label takes precedence over general website guidance.
