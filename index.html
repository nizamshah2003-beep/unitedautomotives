OUR APPROACH

Everything Your Vehicle Needs.

United Automotives India brings together premium pre-owned vehicle solutions, motor insurance and customer support to create a simple, transparent and convenient automotive experience.


Our extensive network and collection of exclusive vehicles give customers the opportunity to explore a wide range of brands — from practical entry and mid-segment cars to premium luxury vehicles from brands such as BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Jaguar and Land Rover.


We believe buying and selling a vehicle should be free of stress and, most importantly, an enjoyable experience. Our team works closely with every customer to understand their requirements and provide personalised solutions.


We also welcome part exchanges and aim to offer competitive prices, allowing our clients to avoid the time and effort involved in marketing their vehicle independently.


Our reputation is built on excellence in serving the needs of our customers before, during and after the sale, with quality-assured inventory and dedicated customer support.
